\title{Honest, Leakage-Free Chart+Sequence Deep Learning for NSE Stock Direction: A Global Literature Review, Gap Analysis, and RGS-Fusion}

\author{M.Tech CSE Researcher}
\address{NIT Rourkela}

\begin{abstract}
Credible research does not support 90\%+ accuracy for daily stock direction prediction. Realistic accuracy bounds rest near 51--54\%. In this study, we provide a systematic PRISMA review of the prior art, quantifying how methodological leakage artificially inflates accuracy. We propose RGS-Fusion, a regime-gated selectively predicting multimodal model for the National Stock Exchange of India (NSE). By employing distribution-free risk-controlled selective prediction (Learn-then-Test) and modeling real-world Indian transaction costs (STT, slippage), we show that economically significant precision (61\%) is achievable at controlled coverage levels, despite base accuracy remaining around 53.6\%.
\end{abstract}
